Are you starting your first year of the engineering cycle (3A) at INSA Hauts-de-France this year? First of all, congratulations! 🎉 A great experience awaits you!

To start, don't hesitate to check out the [how courses and studies work at INSA HdF](/futur-etudiant/starter-packs#how-courses-work) if you haven't already.

## How studies work in the engineering cycle

### Organization by specialties

In 3A, you will choose your **engineering specialty** from those offered at INSA HdF.

### Types of courses

The teaching system consists of **3 types of courses**:

#### CM - Lectures

- Theoretical courses in lecture halls
- Presentation of fundamental concepts
- Note-taking recommended

#### TD - Tutorials

- Practical exercises in small groups
- Applying theory in practice
- Interaction with the teacher

#### TP - Practical Work

- **Important: Labs are graded!**
- Handling equipment and software
- Working in pairs or alone
- Reports to submit

### Evaluation system

#### Exams

- **4 exams per year** (2 per semester)
- Written exams, usually 1.5-2 hours
- Schedule communicated during the period

#### Module system

- Subjects in the same field are **grouped into modules**
- You must pass each module with an **average ≥ 10/20**
- **Semester validated = All modules passed**
- Possible compensation between subjects within the same module

## Tips according to your background

### If you come from CPGE

**Be careful not to relax too much!**

Even if the workload will be **less than in prep school**, INSA is still demanding:

- Work is more spread out over time
- More free time for leisure
- **But** you still need regularity
- Labs and projects require time
- Don't underestimate the exams

**Tip:** Find your new rhythm gradually!

### If you come from INSA prep

- Don't relax either! (continuity from prep)

## Social and friendly life

### Making friends

- **Take part in integration events** organized by the BDE
- Join **associations** that interest you
- Don't hesitate to suggest activities to your classmates
- **Group projects** are a great way to bond

### Study/leisure balance

- More free time than in prep = opportunities to go out!
- **Student parties** organized regularly
- **Sports activities** with the BDS
- **Cultural events** with the BDA

## BDE List - If you want to get involved

### Want to join the BDE?

If you want to **join the Student Office (BDE)**, here’s what you need to know:

#### Important timeline

- **BDE elections:** End of 3rd year (April/May)
- **List formation:** From the **start of 3A** (September/October)

#### "Secret" process

- Lists are formed **discreetly** at the start of the year
- **You must not talk about it to other years** (important rule!)
- Discussions are only between 3A students

#### How to go about it?

- **Get closer to friends** who share your motivation
- Identify people interested in student involvement
- Start thinking about the **projects** you’d like to carry out
- Observe how the current BDE works to understand the stakes

#### Desired profiles

A balanced BDE list needs:

- **Event organizers** (Evenings team)
- **Creatives** (Communication team)
- **Athletes** (BDS)
- **Artists** (BDA)
- **Managers** (Treasurer, Secretary)
