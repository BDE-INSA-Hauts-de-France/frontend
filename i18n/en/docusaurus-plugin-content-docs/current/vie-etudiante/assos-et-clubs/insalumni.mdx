**INSA Alumni Hauts-de-France** supports the graduates of **INSA Hauts-de-France** throughout their professional careers and raises awareness among engineering students during their studies:  

- By **supporting engineering students** through conferences and workshops during their studies  
- By **facilitating the professional integration** of recent graduates  
- By **creating intergenerational connections** between engineers from all graduating classes  
- By **organizing convivial events** (afterworks, meetings, and various gatherings)  
- **INSA Alumni Hauts-de-France also serves as a link** with other alumni networks of the INSA Group schools.
