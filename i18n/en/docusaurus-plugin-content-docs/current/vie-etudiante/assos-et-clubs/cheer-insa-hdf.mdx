## Hi, we're CHEER INSA! We've been the cheerleading club of INSA Hauts-de-France since 2022.

### What is cheerleading?
Cheerleading is a sport that combines **gymnastics**, **dance** and especially **stunts**, which is its main difference from pom-pom. It's not yet very recognized in France but every year new clubs open their doors! **We even had a feature on TF1's 1PM news**:

### What does it take to be a cheerleader?
Cheerleading is **for everyone**! For all genders and all body types! The main quality of a cheerleader is their commitment and motivation because this sport can sometimes be demanding both physically and mentally, but fortunately nothing is insurmountable for a united team! It's a sport that relies heavily on mutual support and team spirit - impossible to do cheerleading alone!

![Image 1](/assos-clubs/cheer-insa/1.webp)

### Ok I'm interested, but for what purpose?
Throughout the year, we participate in **university competitions** such as [Toss](/vie-etudiante/evenements#le-toss) or [H5](/vie-etudiante/evenements#le-h5---high-five). These are always important moments for the club as they represent the culmination of a year's work but also a time to meet and exchange with other cheerleaders! We also sometimes perform at events such as sports matches or INSA celebrations.

![Image 2](/assos-clubs/cheer-insa/2.webp)

### How to join us? Do you need prior experience?
No level required! At the beginning of the year we hold **initiation sessions** because there's little chance you've done it before! Training consists of **two sessions per week**!

Feel free to contact us on Instagram [@cheer_insa_hdf](https://instagram.com/cheer_insa_hdf/)!
