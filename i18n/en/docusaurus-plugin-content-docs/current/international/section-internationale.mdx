# 🌍 GLOBALINSA Hauts-de-France: Bilingual International Section

The **GLOBALINSA** program is a 2-year international track designed to train engineers in a multicultural environment. This bilingual French-English program welcomes a maximum of 30 students (50% French, 50% international).

#### 🎯 Who is GLOBALINSA for?

**French students:**
- Holders of the French Baccalauréat
- Application via **Parcoursup**

**International students:**
- Secondary education diploma obtained abroad
- Contact: **globalinsa@insa-hdf.fr**
- Application via the **INSA Group website**

#### 📚 The Program

**Years 1 and 2:** Bilingual French-English courses
- Small class sizes for tutorials and practical work
- French as a foreign language (FLE) courses for non-French speakers
- Visits to French partner companies

**From 3rd year onwards:** Integration into the standard French engineering curriculum
- Opportunities for internships abroad
- Semester of studies abroad

#### Admission Requirements

- **Required level:** Scientific secondary education diploma
- **Language level:** B1 in both English AND French
- **Assessment:** Mathematics tests and interviews
